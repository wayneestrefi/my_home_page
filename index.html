01 / About me

Curious by nature.
Builder by choice.

I’m curious about how the world works—and even more interested in what I can build from that curiosity. Engineering, code, game design, space, and math give me different ways to ask better questions, solve challenging problems, and turn ideas into something people can actually experience.

Some days I’m focused on solving a challenging problem. Other days I’m exploring the universe, studying game design, or turning a new idea into something real. I enjoy understanding how things work, experimenting with different approaches, and improving each project as I learn. Whether I’m writing code, working through a math problem, or building a game, I’m always looking for the next idea worth exploring.
